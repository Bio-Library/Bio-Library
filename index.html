The Challenge & Our Answer
Problem

A Systemic
Waste Crisis.

Thousands of pediatric bio-samples are discarded daily from routine surgeries — scientifically irreplaceable material lost forever to waste protocols.

Researchers spend months navigating broken, manual supply chains, delaying trials and costing institutions millions.

"Securing high-quality control pediatric bone is usually a bottleneck in our research pipeline. This team made the sourcing process completely seamless and efficient, allowing us to hit our project milestones without a single delay." 

— Senior Scientist, Medical University of South Carolina 

Solution

Pediatric Tissue
Exchange.

I

Collection

Partner with pediatric surgical centers to collect and process otherwise-discarded specimens under rigorous ethical protocols.

II

Annotation

Digitize, annotate, and index each sample — fully searchable by tissue type, patient age, procedure, and biomarker profile.

III

Delivery

Verified researchers request matched samples. 48-hour delivery with full compliance and chain-of-custody documentation.
